IMEI Lookup

An Excel add-in that looks up IMEIs in a table of IMEI ranges.

How to use it

  1. Open the workbook that has your range table, then click IMEI Lookup on the Home tab.
  2. The add-in finds the table by its From IMEI and To IMEI headers. If it can't, open the ⋯ menu and choose Table settings.
  3. Enter one or more IMEIs, one per line, and press Lookup or Ctrl+Enter.
  4. Use Go to row on a result to jump to that row in the sheet.
  5. You can paste a whole email or a messy list: the add-in finds the IMEIs in it. With many IMEIs, results start folded under a summary table.
  6. The ⋯ menu has Table check, which lists overlapping or backwards ranges in your table.

IMEIs can have 14, 15 or 16 digits. A 14-digit IMEI gets its check digit added, and a 16-digit one (IMEISV) has its last two digits replaced by the check digit.

Ask AI (optional)

The Ask AI tab lets you ask questions in plain language. The AI never receives your sheet. It asks the add-in to run lookups on your computer, and only those results are sent. The What was sent to the AI list under the chat shows exactly what left your computer.

Your data

  • The add-in only reads your workbook. It never changes cells.
  • Without Ask AI, nothing is sent anywhere.
  • With Ask AI, your questions and the matching rows are sent to an AI provider (Groq, or Google Gemini as a backup) through a server run by whoever set up this add-in for your team. Provider free tiers may use prompts to improve their products.
  • Settings are stored in your browser on this computer.
